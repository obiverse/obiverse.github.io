How to start

Three steps, once.

Download

Tap Get Obiverse Apps on your Android phone and open the file.

Allow, once

Android asks whether your browser may install apps. Say yes for this one file. Obiverse Apps asks the same once for itself.

Tap Get

Pick an app. It downloads, is checked, and installs. When there is an update, it says Update.

Questions

What people ask first.

Is it safe to allow installing apps?

You allow one app, Obiverse Apps, and nothing else. Before it installs anything it checks the file three ways: the list of apps is signed by us, the file must match the checksum on that list, and the app inside must be signed by its maker’s certificate. If any check fails, nothing is installed. Android then refuses any later update signed by someone else.

Why not just Google Play?

Our apps are on Google Play too, where Play allows them. Obiverse Apps works on phones without Google, for people without a Google account, and the day we ship, not the day a review finishes.

I already have one of these from Google Play.

Keep it. An app is signed by one key, and a copy from Google Play keeps updating there. Obiverse Apps sees it, says so, and leaves it alone.

Will my data survive an update?

Yes. An update installs over the app you have, so its data stays. For a wallet, keep your recovery words written down whatever happens.
