Where the difference goes

Six places. Check each one yourself.

Two laws: every kilogram goes somewhere, and every naira goes somewhere. Whatever breaks them is a leak.

Material given away

Products heavier than your standard weight: raw material you paid for and didn’t charge for.

Check it: Weigh 10 finished products against your standard weight.

Material unexplained

Issued to production but not in finished goods, regrind or scrap.

Check it: Compare last week’s raw material issued with finished goods, regrind and scrap, in kilograms.

Stock that moved

A physical count against the book. The difference is a leak.

Check it: Count your three best-selling products today and compare with the book.

Money owed too long

What each distributor owes, oldest first, and who has gone quiet.

Check it: List what each distributor owes, oldest first, and ask each one to confirm the figure.

Money that never arrived

Payments recorded but not on the bank statement, and money in the bank nobody recorded.

Check it: Tick one week of recorded payments against the bank statement, line by line.

Sales with no paper

Goods sold with no invoice or waybill number to trace them by.

Check it: List last week’s dispatches that have no invoice or waybill number.

Questions

What owners ask.

Why don’t my stock and sales match at month-end?

Because something broke one of two laws: every kilogram goes somewhere, and every naira goes somewhere. Usually it is one of six things: material given away, material unexplained, stock that moved, money owed too long, money that never arrived, or sales with no paper.

Is it theft?

Not always. Products heavier than standard, sales with no paper and payments recorded but never banked all look like missing money too. The checks above tell you which it is.

How long does a leak diagnostic take?

2–3 hours with the records you already keep: bank statements, sales, dispatches, stock and payments. It costs ₦100,000 and is credited in full toward setup if you go ahead within 30 days.
