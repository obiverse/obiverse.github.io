BeeWallet

A self-custody Bitcoin and Lightning wallet. Your number is your wallet: send and receive instantly, anywhere, and see every amount in your own currency.

App StoreAndroid

Inside BeeWallet

Your keys

BeeWallet creates a 12-word recovery phrase that only you hold. No account to create, no email to give.

A number people can pay

Every wallet gets a Mobinumber, a 12-digit number from your wallet's identity, and a Lightning address so anyone can pay you, even while your phone is off.

Instant payments

Pay over Lightning or to a Bitcoin address, scan a QR code, and see every payment in one history.

Your own currency

Amounts show in the money of your country, or in sats if you prefer.

Locked by you

Your PIN seals the wallet on your phone, and Face ID or a fingerprint unlocks it.

Notes and contacts

Encrypted notes and contacts live inside the same wallet, under the same PIN.

IF YOU LOSE YOUR PHONE

Type your 12 words into BeeWallet on any phone and the same wallet comes back: the same balance and the same Mobinumber. On iPhone you can also turn on an iCloud backup, which restores with its own recovery code.

Keep the 12 words written down. Nobody else holds them, so nobody else can bring your wallet back.
