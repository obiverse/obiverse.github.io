Questions

What owners ask first.

What is a leak diagnostic?

In 2–3 hours we go through the records you already keep (bank statements, sales, dispatches, stock and payments) and show you where money is leaking, ranked in naira, with the rows behind each finding. It costs ₦100,000 and is credited in full toward setup if you go ahead within 30 days.

Do my staff have to learn new software?

Not in week one. We import what your systems already produce: Excel, CSV or PDF. Later, staff can record on their phones or by WhatsApp message, one role at a time, and every entry is read back before it is saved.

Do you work outside Nigeria?

Websites, yes: we build for businesses anywhere, priced in naira in Nigeria and in dollars elsewhere. FlowLedger is for businesses in Nigeria, and the owner can be anywhere: many run their business at home from abroad.

Is my business information safe with you?

Your records are used only for your review and stay confidential. They are yours: you can have them back at any time.

How much does a website cost?

₦400,000 in Nigeria, $1,000 elsewhere, for a multi-page site with branding, SEO, Google Maps and a WhatsApp button, live in 7 days. ₦800,000 or $2,000 adds a custom design, payments, an admin panel and analytics. Care is ₦25,000 or $50 a month. You pay 60% to start and 40% at go-live. See every website price.

Do you give discounts?

No. If the budget is tight we reduce the scope, never the price, so what you get is always done properly.
