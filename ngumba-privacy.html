NGUMBA PRIVACY POLICY

Your documents stay on your phone.

Effective: 19 August 2026 · Obiverse LLC

THE SHORT VERSION

  • Ngumba collects nothing, because it sends nothing anywhere
  • There is no account, no sign-up, and no server holding your papers
  • Your documents, invoices and signature never leave the handset
  • No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no crash reporting
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything it held
01

WHAT WE COLLECT

Nothing. Ngumba has no network connection of any kind. It contains no code that can send data anywhere — no server calls, no analytics library, no advertising framework, no crash reporter. There is no account to create and nothing to log in to.

This is not a policy we could quietly change without shipping a different application. It is a property of how the app is built.

02

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR PHONE

Everything: the pages you photograph, the text read from them so you can search, the invoices and receipts you write, your business details and logo, your signature, and your PIN if you set one.

All of it is written into the app's own private storage on the handset and encrypted where it sits. The key is held by the phone's own keystore — the Keychain on iOS, the Android Keystore — and never leaves it.

03

WHAT THE APP ASKS FOR

The camera, to photograph the page you are scanning. Only while you are scanning; there is no background use.

Your photos, and only the single picture you choose through the system picker. Ngumba cannot browse your library.

Face ID or the fingerprint reader, if you turn on unlocking without a PIN. The app never receives your biometrics — the phone answers yes or no.

04

WHEN YOU SEND SOMETHING

Sending hands the finished file to another app you choose — WhatsApp, your mail app, a printer. It does not pass through us on the way, and we never see it.

What happens to that file afterwards is governed by whichever app you handed it to, not by this policy.

05

BACKUPS

Ngumba can write a backup file containing your documents, which you then keep wherever you choose. It is an ordinary zip that any computer can open, and it is not encrypted, so that a backup does not depend on this app still existing to be readable. Treat it as you would a folder of the papers themselves.

Automatic cloud backup by the operating system is switched off, deliberately, so that your documents are not copied to a cloud account without your asking.

06

THE PIN, AND WHAT IT IS FOR

The optional PIN keeps a borrowed or mislaid phone out of your documents. It is held in the phone's keystore alongside everything else.

It is not protection against somebody with the handset, specialist tools and time. We would rather say that plainly than let you assume otherwise.

07

CHILDREN

Ngumba is a tool for running a business and is not directed at children. Since it collects nothing from anybody, it collects nothing from children either.

08

YOUR RIGHTS

Rights to access, correct, export or delete personal data are rights against whoever holds it. We hold none of yours, so there is nothing for us to hand over or erase.

Your own copy is yours: export it with the backup, or remove everything by deleting the app.

09

CHANGES

If a future version of Ngumba ever sends anything anywhere — for example to share a document between your own devices — this page will say so before that version is released, and the app will ask you first.
