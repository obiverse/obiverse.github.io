Inside oSalah

Prayer Times

Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha — calculated locally using the Meeus solar position algorithm. Five methods (MWL, ISNA, Egyptian, Umm al-Qura, Karachi) and both Shafi'i and Hanafi Asr schools.

Qibla Compass

A beautifully crafted compass with Arabic cardinals and Islamic geometric design. Glows and vibrates when you face the Kaaba within 5°. Uses Al-Biruni's great-circle azimuth formula.

Quran Reader

All 114 surahs in clear Uthmani Arabic. Translations in English (Sahih International), French (Hamidullah), and Hausa (Gumi). Adjustable font size. Auto-bookmark.

Dhikr Counter

Digital tasbeeh for post-prayer remembrance. Tap to count, swipe to navigate. 8 dhikr phrases with progress ring and daily tally.

Zakat Calculator

Add your assets, subtract your debts. See your nisab threshold and zakat due at 2.5%.

99 Names of Allah

Contemplate one Beautiful Name each day. Arabic, transliteration, and English meaning. Full grid for browsing all 99.

Hijri Calendar

Islamic lunar calendar with month navigation and upcoming events — Ramadan, Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha, Mawlid, Ashura, and more.
