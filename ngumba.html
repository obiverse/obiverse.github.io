WHERE YOUR DOCUMENTS STAY

On your phone. There is no account, no server holding your papers, and no copy of them anywhere but the handset in your hand.

When you send something it goes where you sent it — WhatsApp, email, a printer — and not through us on the way.

One consequence worth knowing. No cloud means no cloud backup. Ngumba writes a backup file you keep yourself, so send anything irreplaceable to yourself as well.
