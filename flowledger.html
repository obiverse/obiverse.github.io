How it works

Three steps. Nothing new to type.

What it finds

Every leak, ranked in naira.

Two laws: every kilogram goes somewhere, and every naira goes somewhere. Whatever breaks them is a finding. Check the six yourself.

Material given away

Products heavier than your standard weight: raw material you paid for and didn’t charge for.

Material unexplained

Issued to production but not in finished goods, regrind or scrap.

Stock that moved

A physical count against the book. The difference is logged as a leak.

Money owed too long

What each distributor owes, oldest first, and who has gone quiet.

Money that never arrived

Payments recorded but not on the bank statement, and money in the bank nobody recorded.

Sales with no paper

Goods sold with no invoice or waybill number to trace them by.

The details

Sound familiar?

The numbers never meet at month-end.

Raw material goes into production. Products go out to distributors. Money comes back, some of it, some by transfer, some by cash. At month-end the stock, the books and the bank don’t agree, and nobody can say where the difference went.

It went somewhere. FlowLedger finds out where.

On WhatsApp

Your staff already use it.

Staff record on WhatsApp in short messages, like send aba 750 10 wb 41, or by voice note. Every entry is read back and saved only when they reply yes, under their name.

Each person can do only what their job allows: the store records stock, accounts records money, the auditor only asks.

Each finding goes to the person who fixes it. If nobody takes it in 4 hours, it goes to the manager, then to you.

Every day, every month

The owner knows without asking.

7am briefing

Each job gets its own: the store, what is paid and ready to load; production, what paid orders still need; accounts, who owes and the bank.

9pm summary

The day on one message: what the business is worth, money in, who owes, and what needs you. Nothing to log into.

Distributors say YES

Each distributor gets their statement on WhatsApp and replies YES or says what’s wrong. What they owe you becomes agreed, not argued.

The month on one page

Close the month and get one page: worth, made, sold, money in, owed, leaks found and fixed. Plus the VAT view and a pack for your auditor.

Questions

Before you decide.

Who is FlowLedger for?

Businesses that turn inputs into outputs and sell them through others: manufacturers, and the distributors and shops they supply. If materials go in, goods go out and money comes back, FlowLedger can follow it.

My accountant already does this.

Then FlowLedger makes your accountant faster. Every finding shows its source rows, the month closes with its checks, and the auditor pack has the journal, the balance and a sample of entries with the evidence to ask for.

Why not just tell me the price?

Because the price only makes sense next to the leak. The diagnostic tells you what the leak costs you each month. Then we give you two options, out loud, and you choose.
